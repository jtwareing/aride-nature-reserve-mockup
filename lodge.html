CADBURY LODGE · ARIDE ISLAND

A NEW CHAPTER FOR A HISTORIC PLACE

Restored for
the island’s future.

Once known as the Plantation Lodge, Aride’s historic lodge has been extensively reconstructed and reopened as Cadbury Lodge.

The renovation marks a new chapter in the reserve’s conservation story. The lodge is named in recognition of three generations of the Cadbury family’s contribution to conservation in Seychelles. A stay here places you within the working reserve, alongside the island team who care for its wildlife and habitats.

The lodge reopened on 16 December 2025. Details such as room types, facilities, rates and availability should be confirmed directly with the reserve.

SLOW DOWN TO ISLAND TIME

Days shaped
by the sea and sky.

Wake to the sounds of a seabird colony. Spend the day with the reserve’s extraordinary natural heritage. Return to a peaceful lodge set within a protected island landscape.

A stay on Aride is an invitation into a living nature reserve. Please follow the guidance of island staff and respect the wildlife around you.
